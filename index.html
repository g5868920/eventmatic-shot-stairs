How to play
1P (blue cap, green shirt, starts right): ← / →. 2P (red cap, pink shirt, starts left): Z / C. Walk into the other player to push them. On a touch screen the left/right half of the screen moves 1P.
start / restart: press Space / Enter, or tap the button on screen. M mutes / unmutes the sound.
life: spikes on the ceiling or on a floor cost life; landing on a normal floor restores one bar; a beer mug restores more
round over: the first player to run out of life or fall off the bottom loses. The screen shows the loser, both players' floors and the shots: 0.5 per 10 combined floors (see config.js)
